## Requirements

You can source the required components from the [official source list](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1XCATlP0l26rLCiDUOuux1jyK2GdhggXhOg_e36ck4o4/edit?usp=sharing). Quantities, recommended links, and estimated prices are listed in the spreadsheet.

You will also need to get a custom PCB made. We provided the Gerber and Drill files inside the [`assets` folder](https://github.com/Hydle-Research-Group/open-pemf/tree/main/assets/designs/pcb). You can use a service like [JLPCB](https://jlcpcb.com/) or [PCBWay](https://www.pcbway.com/) to get a couple produced for roughly $10. 

A capable 3D printer and PETG filament are recommended tools. We encourage the usage of PETG, as the OpenPEMF enclosure contains electronics that can potentially heat up.
