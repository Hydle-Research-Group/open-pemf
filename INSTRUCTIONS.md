## Requirements

You can source the required components from the [official source list](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1XCATlP0l26rLCiDUOuux1jyK2GdhggXhOg_e36ck4o4/edit?usp=sharing). Quantities, recommended links, and estimated prices are listed in the spreadsheet.

You will also need to produce a custom PCB. We provided instructions for that [here](https://github.com/Hydle-Research-Group/manufacturing/blob/main/processes/P000002-R1).

The following tools and items are highly recommended to complete this project:

- Capable 3D printer with PETG filament
- Soldering iron station
- Hex socket set
- Snips (or other form of cutting tool)
- Wire stripper 
- Wire crimper
- Heat shrink tubing
